Why Do I Need Mentorship?

There are various possible approaches there to trading in Forex. And there is plenty of information available on the web regarding these approaches. Some are of good quality, and some are poor. It’s very easy to get lost in the silos. A good mentor knows what works and which is the fastest way of achieving high profitability.

Basically, there are 2 main approaches to trade forex. One is through Technical Analysis, and the other is through Fundamental Analysis. Technical Analysis is about looking at the price on the chart and marking different points to make a prediction of the price increase or decrease. And Fundamental Analysis is about following external events in the real world that impact the economy and make predictions based on that.

Technical analysis is usually the most common approach adopted by retail traders. Because, Fundamental Analysis needs to have economic knowledge and financial academic background. Technical analysis can be done using some indicators or manually using concepts like SMC (Smart Money Concepts).

Using indicators is an easier approach to make analysis. Most of the time indicators lag, because they need to see the recent price to make the prediction. By the time it has made a prediction the opportunity to place the trade might be gone. Also, each indicator serves a generic purpose and indicates a simpler aspect of the price movement. So, often there is a need to use multiple indicators which makes the workspace clumsy. Also, generally it’s hard to achieve high profitability using the indicators.

The other way of doing technical analysis is using industry standard practice like SMC. SMC helps to understand the underlying reason behind why prices move. The reason prices make bigger moves is because at certain point or time big financial institutes (like banks) place large volumes of orders. There are signs that can be learnt using SMC concepts which help to spot such movements. So, trading along with the institutes increases the chance of the prediction to be right.

The SMC concepts are hard to learn, and some knowledge is simply not very available on the internet. So, it is a better and faster approach to learn Forex trading from a mentor. Some people can be profitable without a mentor in about 3 to 5 years if they put their highest effort and of course in a smarter way. But unfortunately, the harsh reality is that around 95% of retail traders never achieve profitability. So, it is highly recommended that you learn from a good mentor.

Why is Mentorship Expensive?

Ever wonder why finding a great trading mentor feels almost impossible? It all comes down to a classic timing paradox. An exceptional mentor is a successful, seasoned pro who has spent years mastering the markets. But here is how the typical trading journey actually looks: once a trader figures out how to be consistently profitable, it usually takes about two to three years to hit that first million. After that? Their wealth starts compounding exponentially.

Here is the catch:

  • The Early Stage (The Hidden Gem): During a trader's early profitable years, their massive wins aren't always visible to the public yet. Because they are still navigating the financial hurdles of building wealth, they are often highly motivated to mentor others. They know their strategy works and want to share it. Unfortunately, because the flashy lifestyle isn't there yet, most people hesitate to join and miss out on invaluable guidance.
  • The Success Stage (The Closed Door): Once that same trader breaks through and achieves massive financial freedom, everything changes. They want to enjoy the fruits of their labor—not spending their weekends teaching classes. At this point, their time is so incredibly valuable that if they do offer mentorship, the price tag is simply out of reach for most everyday students.

The takeaway? The best time to learn from a master is right before the rest of the world realizes how good they are.
