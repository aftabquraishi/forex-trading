About Aftab Quraishi

From software engineering at top multinational corporations to scaling funded institutional trading systems full-time.

My Background

My name is Aftab Quraishi. I am currently based in Bangladesh. I started my career as a software engineer and worked in the software industry for more than 20 years. Most of the time I worked in top MNC software companies in Bangladesh which are headquartered in the US.

Pay was always good compared to any other job. But a lifelong career as an employee is not very attractive, whether I lived in Bangladesh or moved abroad. The pay is mostly according to the local market living standard. It’s possible to afford an upper-middle-class living standard, but it is difficult to develop financial security. Growth is only 5% to 10% per year, which makes it difficult to cope with the ever-increasing cost of living.

It’s quite possible to build wealth through a software career if someone takes a path of innovation and entrepreneurship. But again, that requires deep passion, desperate dedication, and a very long time. Also, working as an employee is about a lifetime of working in a 9-to-5 job. If someone has a priority for a balanced life, working 8 hours per day would feel very frustrating.

🔄 The Career Shift

I started to desperately look for an alternative. During August 2025, I got to know about Forex through a friend. Realizing the true potential of Forex, I decided to switch my career to Forex trading. I was very lucky to find a very good mentor, so I got the courage to jump into Forex trading full-time.

My Journey with Forex

I spent 2 months learning SMC concepts, 2 months backtesting the strategy (about 1000 trades in 8 months of past data), 2 months demo trading, and now it’s been 4 months of live trading in a 200K funding challenge.

Trading evolved over time, during all phases—backtesting, demo trading, and live trading. Strategy shaped over time. Initially, I took more trades, and gradually increased focus on high-quality setups. It’s still evolving because market conditions change every month. Experience is the key to mastering the market.
