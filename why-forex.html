What is Forex?

Forex is buying and selling foreign currencies (Foreign Exchange). Any form of currency conversion that happens is part of the Forex Market, for example, conversions during import/export, OTC (over the counter) transactions, travel, etc.

It’s a market where about 10 trillion-dollar transactions happen every day, 30 times larger than the New York Stock Exchange (NYSE) and 900 times larger than the London Stock Exchange (LSE).

It’s a decentralized market. So, changes in the exchange rate happen due to many unanticipated factors.

How does it work?

Exchange happens based on currency pairs and the conversion rate of that pair. For example, EURUSD is a pair, and the rate today is 1.13. Which means if you convert 1 Euro you will get 1.13 USD.

This price is tracked at the fifth digit after the decimal point. At one point in the day, the rate might be 1.13245, and after 1 hour it may change to 1.13260. Which means EUR got 15 points stronger than USD. So, if you bought 100,000 EUR and sold after 1 hour, you would have made a profit of 15 EUR in 1 hour.

How do I do Forex trading?

Forex trading can be done online. You can open an account with a broker and deposit your capital. Then use an app like MetaTrader to place the buy/sell orders.

When you place an order, your deposited currency is converted to the base currency. For example, your deposited currency was USD. Then you place a buy order for 1,000 dollars for the GBPJPY pair (GBP is the base currency here). Your 1,000-dollar capital will be converted to an increased amount, for example, 100 times more than the actual. This is called “Leverage”.

Understanding Leverage

Leverage is offered by the broker. Leverage is a way of lending capital to the trader. A 1:100 Leverage means your capital of 1,000 dollars will have a buying power equivalent to 100,000 dollars (1000 x 100).

Why do brokers offer Leverage? It’s because retail traders usually have very small capital to make any reasonable profit. For example, according to the earlier example (15 EUR profit from 100,000 EUR in 1 hour), you would make only 15 cents with a 1,000 dollar deposit. So, this is not very attractive. Because brokers offer Leverage, it feels appealing to people to trade.

If you make a profit, the broker gets a commission. But if you lose, you lose your own capital. The broker doesn’t lose anything. Because they take a commission from every type of order, let it be a buy order or a sell order. Your trade will automatically stop when you run out of capital.

So, coming back to how a trade is executed. Your capital will be leveraged and converted to equivalent GBP and held for a duration until you close the order or your account wipes out. If the price increases, then the trade will close with a profit. In case of a loss, the trade will close after a 1,000-dollar loss (if you have set a fixed stop) or until your account wipes out.

Why is Forex Trading such a great career option?

Forex Trading is a great career option for multiple reasons:

  • Less capital needed: Less capital is needed to make a high profit. This is possible due to the “Leverage” offered by the broker.
  • Capital is optional (Prop Firms): There are funding companies who will provide a very good amount of capital for a very small fee. These are called Prop Firms. To obtain capital, you must first prove your trading skill by making a reasonable amount of profit (usually 10% to 15%) by participating in a challenge. The firm will provide you with a demo account with a balance, for example, 100K USD. You have to pay a small fee to participate in the challenge. The fee might be around 1K USD for 100K challenges. Once you pass the challenge, they will pay back the fee you paid to participate. So, it’s possible to build your trading career without substantial trading capital.
  • Exponential Growth: A highly competent trader makes about 5% to 10% profit per month. Which is about 60% to 120% profit in a year. So, the capital usually grows double every year.
    • Generally, any business is considered profitable if it generates at least 30% profit in a year. A doubling profitable business is rare.
    • An investment usually returns about 8% to 10% profit, for example, a Fixed Deposit in a bank.
    • A job usually provides a 5% to 10% increase in salary every year.
  • Ultimate Freedom: Trading will give you the ultimate freedom. It will provide you with three types of freedom - money, time, and location. The financial growth it will provide you from the second or third year onward will be unmatched by any other career option. Once you achieve competency, you will need to trade for about 3 to 6 hours per day, 5 days a week. You take your own days off. There is no boss, no permission needed. You can trade from anywhere in the world; you just need a laptop with an internet connection.
  • Fast Career Build-up: It takes about 1 to 2 years to start earning a decent amount of money (5K to 10K USD) to afford a good living standard almost anywhere in the world. This is very low compared to most other career options. To earn a handsome salary in any discipline, you have to get a bachelor’s degree (4 years) and 3 to 4 years of experience at least. So, to be a doctor or engineer, 7 to 8 years of time investment is needed to start a good earning. I would not say it’s easy to learn Forex trading. But, in my opinion, it’s a lot easier than any other job or business.
